What is included in every package?

Every package includes full usage rights, one round of revisions, and delivery 14 days after the product arrives. Raw footage is available on request.

  • Full usage rights on every channel, with no licence fees or renewals.
  • One round of revisions.
  • Raw footage on request.
  • A written scope confirmed before anything ships.
  • We post nothing. Your content stays yours.

Which package fits?

One product for one listing fits the single-product package. A product line fits three products in one shoot, which is the lowest cost per product. A hero commercial with cast and several locations fits a production day.
