How do you film an e-bike?

E-bike films are shot with real riders on real roads, paths and beaches, with no green screen. The shoot covers riding footage, close detail of the motor, battery, display and folding, and lifestyle scenes with the rider.

Questions about e-bike video production

What e-mobility brands ask before shipping a bike.

How many bikes do we need to ship?

One unit per model. Where two riders appear together, as in the KingBull and Freedare films, ship two.

Do you supply the riders?

Yes. Riders are cast to match the person who buys the bike. Production days include cast talent.

How long does it take?

14 days from the day the bike arrives at the studio to final files.

Can the film be used on Amazon, our site and ads?

Yes. You get full usage rights on every channel and cutdowns sized for each.

We are a Chinese brand. Can we work remotely?

Yes. See US video production for Chinese brands.
