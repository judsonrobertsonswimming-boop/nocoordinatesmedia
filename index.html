08 / Questions

Before you ask.

How much does a product video cost?

A single product starts at $1,500 for a 45-second film and 15 photos. Three products in one shoot is $2,500. A full production day with cast talent and crew is $3,000. Anything outside those gets a written quote first.

Do you film corporate, law firm and medical videos?

Yes. Alongside product films, No Coordinates Media shoots company and brand films, attorney and firm videos, and videos for medical practices. These are filmed at your office or on location in South Florida and quoted by the production day.

Where do you film?

Across Miami-Dade and Broward, including Miami and Fort Lauderdale. Product brands anywhere in the world ship one unit to the studio.

How long does it take?

Product films take 14 days from the day your product arrives at the studio to final files. Other shoots get a delivery date in the written quote.

Who owns the footage?

You do. Full usage rights on every channel, with no licence fees or renewals. Raw footage is available on request.

Are you influencers? Will you post it?

No. We're a production studio, not creators or affiliates. We post nothing and take no commission. You get the files and run them wherever you sell.

We're based outside the US. Does that work?

Yes. Many of our clients are brands based in China that sell in the US. You ship one unit to our studio and everything else happens by email, WhatsApp or WeChat. The film is shot in the US, with people and locations your American buyers recognize.

What if we want changes?

One round of revisions is included in every package.
