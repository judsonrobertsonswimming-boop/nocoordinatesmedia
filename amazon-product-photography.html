Why shoot product photos in the US?

Shoppers on Amazon US notice when the people and places in a photo aren't American. Every set is shot in South Florida with American models in real locations.

Nothing is shot abroad or built from stock backgrounds.

How does it work?

A product photo set takes five steps and 14 days from the day the product arrives at the studio to final files. You approve the shot list before anything is shot.

  1. Send the listing. Tell us the product and who buys it. You get a written price and shoot plan within one business day.
  2. Ship one unit. It goes to the studio in South Florida.
  3. Approve the shot list. We write it and you sign off before anything is filmed.
  4. We film. Days 1 to 7: casting, locations and the shoot, with the product in real use.
  5. You get the files. By day 14: the video, cutdowns and 15 photos in a shared folder. One round of revisions is included.

Questions about product photography

Short answers to what sellers ask before they ship a product.

Can I add a video later?

Yes. Photos and video are cheapest when shot together, so ask for both prices up front.

Do you supply the models?

Yes. American models are cast in South Florida to match the buyer you describe.

Who owns the photos?

You do. Full usage rights on every channel, with no licence fees or renewals.

Our brand is outside the US. Does that work?

Yes. You ship one unit and everything else happens by email, WhatsApp or WeChat. See US video production for Chinese brands.
