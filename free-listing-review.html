What is in the free listing review?

The free listing review is a written review of one Amazon listing. It covers the main images, the video, the bullets and the A+ content: what works, what we would change, and why it matters for conversion.

Part of the listingWhat the review covers
Main imagesOrder, clarity at thumbnail size, lifestyle versus studio, what a buyer cannot tell from the photos.
VideoWhether there is one, what it shows in the first seconds, and what is missing.
BulletsWhether the first two bullets answer the buyer's main question.
A+ contentWhether it adds proof or repeats the bullets.

How does it work?

You send one Amazon listing link and an email address. The written review arrives by email within a few days. There is no product to ship, no call and no obligation.

  • Send one Amazon listing link.
  • We review it against the product videos and photo sets we make every week.
  • You get the written review by email.

Who is it for?

The free listing review is for brands selling on Amazon US that want an outside view of a listing before paying for new video or photos.

If the review shows the listing needs a film, the Amazon product video package starts at $1,500.
