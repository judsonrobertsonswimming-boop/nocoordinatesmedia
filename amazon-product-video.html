What moves the price between $1,500 and $3,000?

Three things move the price: how many actors are on screen, how many locations we film in, and how many different scenes the video needs. One actor in one location is the $1,500 package.

A family at a house plus a beach scene sits closer to $3,000. Photos without a video cost less, and several products filmed in one shoot are discounted: three products are $2,500 in total.

Why film an Amazon product video in the US?

Shoppers on Amazon US are looking at American homes, roads and beaches every day, and they notice when a video was filmed somewhere else. Every No Coordinates Media video is filmed in South Florida with American actors in real locations.

Nothing is shot abroad and dressed to look American. That is what the price pays for, and it is the difference between this package and a lower-priced studio or stock video.

How does it work?

An Amazon product video takes five steps and 14 days from the day the product arrives at the studio to final files. You approve the shot list before anything is filmed.

  1. Send the listing. Tell us the product and who buys it. You get a written price and shoot plan within one business day.
  2. Ship one unit. It goes to the studio in South Florida.
  3. Approve the shot list. We write it and you sign off before anything is filmed.
  4. We film. Days 1 to 7: casting, locations and the shoot, with the product in real use.
  5. You get the files. By day 14: the video, cutdowns and 15 photos in a shared folder. One round of revisions is included.
