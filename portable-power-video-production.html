How do you film a power station?

Power station films show the unit doing real work outdoors: running appliances at a campsite, a beach or a backyard, with people using it. The shoot also covers ports, display and charging in close detail.

  • Use scenes: camping, beach, tailgate, home backup.
  • Detail shots: ports, display, handles, solar and wall charging.
  • Real people plugging in real devices, not a render.
  • Photos from the same setups for the listing and A+ content.
