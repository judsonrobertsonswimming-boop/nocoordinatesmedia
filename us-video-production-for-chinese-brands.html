For brands in China

US-based product video and photography for Chinese brands.

No Coordinates Media is a US video production studio in South Florida that films product videos and photos for Chinese brands selling in America. The brand ships one unit to the studio, the film is shot with American talent and locations, and files are delivered in 14 days. Packages start at $1,500.

By Judson Robertson, Founder / DirectorPublished November 19, 2026Updated November 19, 2026

Why do Chinese brands film in the US?

Chinese brands film in the US so the listing shows American people, homes, roads and beaches that US buyers recognise. No Coordinates Media shoots every film on location in South Florida with local cast.

  • American talent using the product on camera.
  • Real US locations: neighbourhoods, beaches, parks, campsites.
  • A US production studio, not an influencer. We post nothing and take no commission.
  • Many of our clients are brands based in China that sell in the US.

How does it work from China?

The brand ships one unit to the studio in Florida and approves a shot list by email or WeChat. The film is shot in the US and final files are delivered 14 days after the product arrives.

Day 0

Ship

You send one unit to the studio and a short note on who buys it. We write the shot list and send it for your sign-off.

Days 1 to 7

Shoot

We cast, scout and film in Miami and South Florida. Real people, real locations, the product in use.

By day 14

Deliver

Film, cutdowns and photos land in a shared folder, sized for Amazon, your site and paid social. One revision round is included.

The studio shipping address is sent with the written quote. Everything else is handled by email, WhatsApp or WeChat.

Questions from Chinese brands

Short answers to what brand teams ask first.

Who owns the video and photos?

The brand. Full usage rights on Amazon, your website, TikTok, ads and anywhere else, with no licence fees.

Are you an influencer or KOL?

No. No Coordinates Media is a production studio. We do not post the content and we do not take affiliate commission.

Do you offer free shooting for a sample?

For products that retail over $500, yes: ship one unit, we shoot the film and photos, and you keep every file. Other products use the paid packages.

How many revisions are included?

One round of revisions is included in every package.

Can we see our listing reviewed first?

Yes. Send one Amazon link for a free written listing review.
