About No Coordinates Media.

No Coordinates Media is a video production studio in South Florida founded and directed by Judson Robertson. The studio films product videos and photography, corporate and brand films, and videos for law firms and medical practices, and has produced work for KingBull, Pecron, Gotrax, NIU and BLUETTI.

By Judson Robertson, Founder / DirectorPublished November 19, 2026Updated November 19, 2026

What does the studio film?

No Coordinates Media films product videos and photography, corporate and brand films, law firm videos, medical practice videos, and website and social video. The studio works across Miami-Dade and Broward and with product brands worldwide.

Who has the studio filmed for?

The studio's work includes films and photography for KingBull, Pecron, Gotrax, NIU, Lion Energy, Freedare, Magicycle, BLUETTI, REVIX, Dunlop and NASCAR Store, along with hospitality, automotive and apparel shoots.
